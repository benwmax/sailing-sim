What I saw at 1:1:

- **Housed:** the near row's guns are housed, with the barrels angled up and resting back at the breech, as a housed gun is lashed muzzle-up. The far row sits further off, with the same stance.
- **The aprons:** each near gun has a **flat, dark, square plate lying over the breech end**. It reads as the lead apron, and it's clearly there on every near gun in the 3x crop.
- **The lids:** at each gun's **muzzle end there's a larger flat rectangular plate standing upright**. It reads as the port lid. It's most visible in the close still, where every gun carries one beside the muzzle.
- **Tompions:** the barrel ends show a dark round face, which could be the tompion, but at this size I can't tell it apart from the barrel's open end.
- **The 28 lid hinges can't be seen:** there's no distinct hinge geometry at either distance.
- **Placeholder surfaces:** the shapes are blocky and plain: box carriages, cylinder barrels, disc trucks. In the close crop the lids and carriage sides carry **a fine checker pattern**, like the engine's default grid material. The gun meshes may not have their final materials on this stage.
- **The close still's framing:** the camera ended up at water level, below the stage. The guns seem to float about 2 m above the water there, so they're seen from underneath, against the sky. A camera target a little higher (for example z 3 to 4) would look down onto the lids and aprons.
