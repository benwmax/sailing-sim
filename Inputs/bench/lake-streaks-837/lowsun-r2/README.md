# Lake streaks at low sun, into the sun: d8f2a3dc, a3f81854, a3f81854 -SailingNoWindrows (PC, Nov 26 2026)

The reshoot of `../lowsun/`, with the camera facing the sun.

**Times and courses:** 12 kn, AfternoonBreeze from the south.
- **08:00:** the sun about 120 degrees, at TWA +60 (starboard), heading about 120, into the sun.
- **16:30:** the sun about 235 degrees, at TWA -60 (port), heading about 240, into the sun.
- I took the sun bearings for late November on the lake, and set the tack so her heading points at the sun.

**Cameras:**
- the default chase camera at `-SailingCamYaw=0`, astern and looking forward, so into the sun;
- the 30 m `up30` shot from the 2 PM set: 20 m astern, 30 m up, looking forward and down.

**Places:** the club (launch 1) and 3 km out. **Builds:** the same three. **Clips:** 24, 10 s at 1080p.

**Files:**
- `compare-ls2-<hour>-<place>-<camera>.webp` puts d8f2a3dc, a3f81854 and a3f81854 without windrows side by side.
- `clips/` has all 24 mp4s.

## What they show

**No distinct bright streaks in any build, at either time, from either camera.**
- **Chase, into the sun:** a strong sun path with glitter breaking along the wave crests. The glitter is the same width
  and pattern in all three builds. #837's windrows don't separate out as white lines, and turning them off
  (`-SailingNoWindrows`) changes nothing visible.
- **up30:** golden, sun-lit chop in a top-down view, identical across the three builds.
- **The only build difference you can see** is #834's birds over the horizon in the a3f81854 frames.

**Reading:**
- At 12 kn, into the sun or across it, at 08:00 or 16:30, the windrows #837 adds don't show as the bright white streaks
  Bean saw.
- So either those streaks come from something else, maybe a particular sea state, camera height or exposure, or they
  need a different wind or view.
- Bean's own time and view, or a still of what he saw, would let me match it directly.
