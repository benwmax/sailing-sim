# Shore "before" stills, Nov 16 2026 (PC)

These are today's Ray Hubbard shore at close range: eight stills on main **8f5331f4**, the "before" set for the shoreline plan. They were taken uncooked through `scripts\windows\run-game.ps1` from a fresh worktree on Bean's RTX 4090, with no package.

- **Settings:** Ultra at 1920x1080, `-SailingCapture=Drone` (eye 2 m above the water, 80 deg), 15:00, AfternoonBreeze (fair cumulus).
- **Cameras:** fixed with `-SailingCamWorld` relative to a boat placed out of frame (`-SailingStartAt`, `-SailingKeepStart`). They are 5 to 25 m from the subject, much nearer than the October near-shore audit's 120 to 300 m.
- **Shots:** clubhouse and lawn, the club riprap, the audit's residential shore, one lot with its bulkhead and boat lift, a private dock and boat sheds, the audit's wooded spit, a grass bank north of the club, and the I-30 piers at the west landfall.
- **Files:** `args.csv` has each shot's full command line and spot. The JPEGs are quality 90.
- **Framing:** I checked each still at 1:1 for framing, and the horizon is level in all of them. Shot 4 is a third take; the first two put the camera inside a boathouse.
- **Saved:** Bean's Saved was held aside during the runs and restored (profile hash B57AA3AF).
