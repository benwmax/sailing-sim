Sailing tip · 7 of 42

Leaving a slip with the wind behind you

Back out under bare poles or the motor, turn the bow away from the docks, and raise the main only once you are head to wind in open water. Raising it in the slip with the wind behind you will drive you into the dock.

Rebinding

Select a key or button, then press the new one. Esc or Ⓑ cancels. A key used twice in the same context turns orange, and you choose which action keeps it.

Bindings are saved per device, so the Mac and the PC can differ.
