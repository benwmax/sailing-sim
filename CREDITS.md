# Credits and licenses

## Sound

Every sound in the game is synthesized at runtime in code (`Source/SailingSim/Private/SailingAudio.cpp`) from
filtered noise and simple oscillators: wind in the rigging, luffing and flogging sails, telltale flutter, water
past the hull and the rumble near hull speed, bow-wave slaps, winch clicks, sheets running out, rain and thunder.
Since Graphics G7 a sample pack (`Inputs/audio`, imported to `/Game/Audio/Pack`) plays in place of most of those voices
where it is imported: script-built loops and one-shots made for this project (CC0) and three recordings from Wikimedia
Commons, credited in `Audio/Samples/CREDITS.md`: "Gull 1.ogg" by avphillips (public domain), "Larus atricilla.ogg" by
Tony Phillips (CC BY 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0) and "Rain on a veranda and t.ogg" by ezwa (public
domain), each cut, filtered and normalised. The synthesized voices remain for gusts, telltales, rain on the water, the
squall, thunder and the diesel, and for anything not imported.

The pink-noise filter coefficients follow Paul Kellet's widely published public-domain "refined" pink noise method.

## Art and content

- Hull, rig, sails, crew: built in code from J/22 class dimensions (placeholder art until a later phase).
- Materials (`/Game/Materials`): generated by `scripts/make_materials.py`.
- Engine content used under the Unreal Engine EULA: basic shapes (cube, cylinder, plane), the sky atmosphere,
  and the volumetric cloud material `/Engine/EngineSky/VolumetricClouds/m_SimpleVolumetricCloud_Inst`.

## Data

- J/22 reference polar: ORC VPP polar (median of 13 ORC certificates) as tabulated by
  [keelindex.com](https://keelindex.com/boat/j-22/performance), cross-checked against the
  [ORC 2021 J/22 one-design certificate](https://data.orc.org/public/od/2021/j22.od.html). Used for validation only.
- Lake Ray Hubbard shoreline, docks, slips, breakwaters, bridges, buildings and land use:
  © [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) contributors, available under the
  [Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/). The baked files in
  `Content/TerrainData` are a derived database and are offered under the same license. The credit is also shown in
  the game's settings menu.
- Land elevation: U.S. Geological Survey, 3D Elevation Program (3DEP), 1/3 arc-second DEM (public domain).
- Lake bottom: Texas Water Development Board, 2015 Volumetric and Sedimentation Survey of Lake Ray Hubbard,
  interpolated bottom points (public information).
- Pool elevation and gauge level: Texas Water Development Board, Water Data for Texas (public information).
- Fleet particulars and reference polars (Phase 4): class associations and builders (ILCA, International Sunfish
  Class, Catalina, Hobie, Corsair Marine, Lagoon), ORC public certificate data (data.orc.org), keelindex.com,
  Day (2017) "Performance prediction for sailing dinghies" (Ocean Engineering), the Hobie Hotline 1978 polar, and
  boat reviews (SAIL, Yachting Monthly, 48North). Every number and its source URL is in `docs/phase-4/fleet-data.json`.
- Fetch-limited wave growth: U.S. Army Corps of Engineers Shore Protection Manual / JONSWAP relations (public).
- Galveston Bay chart (Phase 5): NOAA Office of Coast Survey Electronic Navigational Charts (ENC, S-57), cells
  US4TX1MJ, US4TX1MK, US4TX1NJ, US4TX1NK, US5HOUAG-AI, US5HOUBF-BI, US5HOUCF, US5HOUCI, US5HOUDG, US5HOUDH, US5TX3GI and
  US5TXTDA-EC (charts.noaa.gov; public domain). Depths, shoreline, aids to navigation and their light characteristics,
  landmarks and magnetic variation are taken from them. The in-game chart is drawn from this data and is not for
  navigation.
- Tides and currents: NOAA Center for Operational Oceanographic Products and Services (CO-OPS), harmonic constituents,
  datums and predictions for stations 8771450, 8771341, 8771013, 8770613, 8771486 and current stations ACT8856 and
  ACT8851 (tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov; public domain). NOAA's published predictions are kept as the test reference.
- Galveston Bay land elevation: USGS 3DEP 1/3 arc-second DEM (public domain). Buildings, land use, piers and marinas:
  © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL, as above).
- Chart symbology follows U.S. Chart No. 1 (NOAA / NGA, public domain). Sun times were checked against published
  Galveston sunrise and sunset tables.
- Navigation Rules (Phase 6): U.S. Coast Guard, Navigation Rules and Regulations Handbook (International and Inland
  Rules; public domain). Racing: rule numbers from World Sailing's Racing Rules of Sailing are cited; no rule text is
  reproduced.
- Gulf passage (Phase 7): NOAA ENC cells US3TX1DC, US4TX1KI-KJ, US4TX1LI-LK, US4TX1MI-MK, US5FPOBB-BC, US5FPOCA-CC,
  US5FPODA-DB, US5TXTDB-DF and US5TXTEC-EE (charts.noaa.gov; public domain), including offshore platforms as
  landmarks; tides from CO-OPS stations 8771510, 8771341, 8771972 and 8772447 (public domain). No land DEM is used
  for the open coast (land is flat above mean high water).
- Crew idles (Crew C1b): motion capture from the CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database, trials 13_04, 82_05,
  114_05, 137_28, 139_01, 139_02, 140_06 and 140_07, baked into small joint motions in the game's code. The data used
  in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217.
- Ocean waves: Tessendorf (2001) "Simulating Ocean Water"; JONSWAP spectrum (Hasselmann et al. 1973); added
  resistance in waves after Gerritsma and Beukelman (1972). Methods only; no code reproduced.
