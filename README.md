# SailingSim

A technically accurate sailing simulator built in Unreal Engine 5.8. First boat: J/22. Home water: Lake Ray Hubbard, Texas, sailing from Rush Creek Yacht Club in Heath.

The research and phased plan live in the Claude Doc "Sailing Game: Research and Phased Plan". Phases so far:
0 (project and build loop), 1 (J/22 physics), 2 (wind, waves and weather), 3 (the home lake).

## Layout

| Path | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `SailingSim.uproject` | C++ project, Blank template, no starter content. Plugins: Enhanced Input, Water, Python (editor only) |
| `Source/SailingPhysics` | Boat, wind, wave, weather and lake-bed physics. Plain C++ (no Unreal), builds standalone |
| `Source/SailingSim` | Game module: pawn, game mode, HUD, water, sky, sound, lake terrain and drills |
| `Content/TerrainData` | Baked Lake Ray Hubbard: heightmap, tree density, docks, bridges, buildings (LFS; staged with the game) |
| `Tools/terrain` | The bake: `build_rayhubbard.py` and `overpass.sh` (see "Rebuilding the lake" below) |
| `Source/SailingSimTests` | Automation tests (editor only) |
| `Content/Maps/Lake.umap`, `Content/Materials` | Generated by `scripts/make_lake_map.py` and `scripts/make_materials.py` |
| `scripts/` | Build, test, map, package and benchmark scripts |
| `Build/` | Unreal's per-platform build folder (generated, ignored). Packaged apps go to `Packaged/`, never here |

## Build, test, package

```sh
scripts/build.sh          # UnrealBuildTool, SailingSimEditor Mac Development
scripts/test.sh           # automation tests headless; report in Artifacts/TestReport
scripts/make-content.sh   # regenerate the materials and Content/Maps/Lake.umap from scripts/*.py
scripts/package-mac.sh    # RunUAT BuildCookRun to Packaged/Mac/SailingSim.app
scripts/bench-mac.sh 20   # run the packaged app for 20 s, print fps, save a screenshot
```

## Controls

Press **?** (controller: View) in the game for the controls card and **Tab** (controller: Menu) for settings.
Keys are rebindable: see `USailingInputSettings` in `Source/SailingSim/Public/SailingSettings.h`.

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Controller |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tiller (bow left / right) | A / D or Left / Right | Left stick |
| Mainsheet trim / ease | W / S | Right / left trigger |
| Jib sheet trim / ease | E / Q | Right / left bumper |
| Crew hike out / in | F / V | D-pad up / down |
| Wing-on-wing | G | X |
| Back the jib (heave to) | B | Left stick press |
| Dock lines on / off (under 1.5 kn, next to a dock) | T | Right stick press |
| Full layout only: traveler, vang, cunningham, outhaul, backstay | Z / C, 1-2, 3-4, 5-6, 7-8 | D-pad left / right, A / B |
| Learning mode, difficulty, instruments, reset | L, K, H, R | Y |
| Settings menu, controls card | Tab, ? | Menu, View |
| Orbit / zoom camera | Mouse, wheel | Right stick |

**Simple** layout (the default, and always on Easy): you steer, trim both sheets and hike; the crew handles the
traveler and sail shape. **Full** gives you every control. **Trim assist** (its own setting) lets the crew trim the
sheets whenever you leave them alone for 4 seconds. Learning mode shows the wind diagram, point of sail, trim and
weather tips; game mode hides them. The physics is the same in every combination.

## Weather and time

In the settings menu (Tab): **Weather** (calm morning, afternoon breeze, overcast, rain, thunderstorm, random day),
**Start time** (06:00 to 20:00, local solar time at Lake Ray Hubbard), **Weather seed** (same seed, same gusts,
shifts and lightning), **Graphics quality** (Low / Medium / High) and **Volume**. The thunderstorm plays in about nine
minutes: towering cells build to the west-northwest, the gust front crosses the water (watch for the dark band and
whitecaps) with a sharp shift and gusts near 30 knots, rain follows a minute later with lightning and thunder at the
speed of sound, then it clears to a light northwesterly.

## The home lake

Lake Ray Hubbard is built from public data (see `CREDITS.md`): USGS 3DEP elevation for the land, the Texas Water
Development Board 2015 volumetric survey for the bottom, and OpenStreetMap for the shoreline, docks, slips,
breakwaters, bridges, buildings and woods. Map frame: north is +X, east is +Y, origin at Rush Creek Yacht Club,
Z = 0 at the conservation pool (435.5 ft NGVD29).

- **Launch spot** (settings menu): Rush Creek Yacht Club, open water off Rush Creek, the south lake near the dam,
  Chandlers Landing, and the north lake below the SH-66 causeway.
- **Lake level**: full pool, or the TWDB gauge reading baked in on the bake date (432.3 ft on Oct 11, 2026, 3.2 ft
  low). The bed rises by the difference, so shoals and banks show the way they do that day. Applies at the next start.
- **Drills** (settings menu, Left/Right starts one), worded as in ASA 101: leave the dock, sail a triangle, heave to,
  crew overboard (figure-eight and quick-stop), return and dock. The drill panel shows the steps and ticks them off.
- **Hazards**: you can run aground (the HUD warns under 1 m below the keel), hit docks and breakwaters, and the
  bridges stop the mast (only the hull fits under). Woods and bluffs cast wind shadows that recover over 2 to 25
  obstacle heights downwind, and the chop grows with the open-water fetch upwind.

## Rebuilding the lake

```sh
python3 -m venv Tools/venv && Tools/venv/bin/pip install pyshp pyproj numpy scipy pillow tifffile
Tools/terrain/fetch_rayhubbard.sh    # USGS DEM, TWDB survey and levels, OpenStreetMap into Data/RayHubbard/raw (git-ignored)
Tools/venv/bin/python Tools/terrain/build_rayhubbard.py   # writes Content/TerrainData; rerun to pick up a new gauge level
```

## Known gaps

- Docks, buildings and trees are simple shapes; the clubhouse and ramp are boxes from OpenStreetMap outlines.
- Depths inside the marina basins come from the nearest TWDB survey point, so slips read shallower than they are;
  the leave-the-dock berth is chosen where the survey shows at least 2.3 m.
- No other boats are moored in the slips yet (traffic comes in Phase 6).
- The shoreline does not move with the lake level beyond what the survey shows: at the gauge level, the 3 ft band
  above the water comes from interpolated survey points and the 3DEP surface, not a shoreline survey.
- Bridge piers are drawn every 30 m along the deck but only the deck is a collision obstacle; the hull passes
  through piers.

- Storm sky reads light grey rather than menacing; no towering cell is visible on the horizon before the front.
- Gust front and cat's paws are visible but subtle from a low camera; learning mode tips point them out.
- Without a spinnaker the J/22 can surf to about 14 kn in 30 kn gusts, which is optimistic; resistance past Fn 0.8 is flat.
- High quality tier dips just under 60 fps in clear breezy afternoons on the M5 Pro.
- Sound is synthesized and not yet tuned by ear; it has not been checked on headphones.
- The downwind polar reference is an approximation (no spinnaker yet); boat art is still boxes.
