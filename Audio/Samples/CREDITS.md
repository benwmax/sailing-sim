## Sample pack (Graphics G7, imported from `Inputs/audio/Samples` to `/Game/Audio/Pack`)

Script-built files (`source: script` in `Inputs/audio/manifest.json`) were made for this project by
`Inputs/audio/scripts/synth.py` and are released CC0. Recorded files are cut, trimmed, filtered and level-normalised
from these Wikimedia Commons recordings (added 2026-10-14):

- **Gull 1.ogg** by avphillips, Public domain, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gull_1.ogg. Modified (cut, filtered, normalised, resampled). Used in: `Birds/gull_call_01.wav`, `Birds/gull_call_02.wav`, `Birds/gull_call_03.wav`
- **Larus atricilla.ogg** by Tony Phillips, [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0), https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Larus_atricilla.ogg. Modified (cut, filtered, normalised, resampled). Used in: `Birds/laughing_gull_01.wav`
- **Rain on a veranda and t.ogg** by ezwa, Public domain, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rain_on_a_veranda_and_t.ogg. Modified (cut, filtered, normalised, resampled). Used in: `Weather/rain_on_roof_light_loop.wav`

Credits screen line: Sound effects include recordings from Wikimedia Commons by Tony Phillips (CC BY 3.0).
